# Risk Governance & Transactional Audit Ledger Specification\n## ICT 0DTE Multi-Strategy Algorithmic Trading Platform\n\n### 1. Risk Management Framework\n* **Concurrency Ceilings:** Strictly limits active portfolio exposure to 1 to 2 concurrent open positions during high-velocity 0DTE sessions.\n* **Emergency Overrides:** Streamlit dashboard equipped with Google Authenticator TOTP MFA protection for emergency bot pauses and cancellation of all open orders.\n\n### 2. Transactional SQLite Audit Ledger (`data/trades.db`)\nEvery signal generation, order routing, and fill is recorded immutably via transactional schemas supporting `strategy_type` (CREDIT_SPREAD or DEBIT_SPREAD) and execution states.
