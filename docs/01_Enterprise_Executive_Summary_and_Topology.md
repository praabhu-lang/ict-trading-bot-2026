# Enterprise Executive Summary & Strategy Topology\n## ICT 0DTE Multi-Strategy Algorithmic Trading Platform\n\n### 1. Business Objective & Scope\nThe ICT 0DTE Multi-Strategy Algorithmic Trading Platform is an autonomous, institutional-grade quantitative execution system designed for intraday volatility harvesting and defined-risk options trading. Operating across zero-days-to-expiration (0DTE) lifecycles, the platform eliminates single-strategy rigidities by dynamically allocating between **Credit Spreads** (capturing accelerated theta decay in range-bound or mean-reverting regimes) and **Debit Spreads** (capturing momentum breakouts around Volume Profile migration nodes).\n\n### 2. Core Architectural Pillars\n* **Multi-Strategy Hybridization:** Simultaneous runtime evaluation of short-premium credit spreads (Bull Put / Bear Call) and directional debit spreads (Bull Call / Bear Put).\n* **Multi-Ticker Universe:** Asynchronous parallel scanning across 10–20 top-tier S&P 100 liquid equities and index benchmarks (SPX, SPY, QQQ, AAPL, MSFT, NVDA, TSLA, AMZN, GOOGL, META, AMD, NFLX, JPM, UNH, V, HD).\n* **Strict Risk Governance:** Portfolio-wide concurrency limits (max 1–2 active positions), minimum option contract cost threshold ($0.70+), dynamic 50% profit-taking trailing stops, and hard market timing cutoffs.\n* **Persistent Audit Logging:** Transactional SQLite/PostgreSQL audit ledgers (`data/trades.db`) combined with GCS state persistence and real-time Streamlit telemetry.
